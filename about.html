About

A good ID(ea) is an initiative with the goal of introducing an (optional) physical student card for UU students.

This policy is a cheap way to reduce unnecessary independence on smartphones devices which have been shown to be deleterious to student mental health and an obstacle in the learning process. Moreover, our research shows that students overwhelming favour this proposal.

For comments and questions, email agoodidea@vjbe.net or find us on Instagram @agoodidea.
